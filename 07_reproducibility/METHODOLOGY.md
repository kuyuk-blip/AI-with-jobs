# Methodology notes

## Why this pack separates three evidence layers
1. **Project dataset** — salary/job structure inside the supplied sample.
2. **External quantitative evidence** — labour-market, exposure, productivity and skills research.
3. **Qualitative evidence** — mechanisms, experience and organisational context.

These layers answer different questions and must not be merged into one causal story.

## Adjusted salary model
The included model is an OLS regression on log salary among 2024–2025 full-time observations assigned to one of six conservative role archetypes.

Controls:
- experience level
- work year
- employee residence
- work mode
- company size

Heteroskedasticity-robust HC1 standard errors are used.

This model is for **descriptive adjustment**, not causal identification.

## Why the role classifier is conservative
A title string does not always identify an occupation reliably. Therefore generic titles are left in Other/general instead of being guessed. The exact mapping is included so users can audit or revise it.

## Reproducibility
All platform-facing dataset values should be regenerated by the included script if the raw CSV changes.
