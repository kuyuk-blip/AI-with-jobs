# Source notes

## S01 — International Labour Organization (ILO)
**Generative AI and Jobs: A Refined Global Index of Occupational Exposure**  
Date: 2025-05-20  
Type: Working paper / task-level occupational exposure  
Geography: Global  
URL: https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure  
Method note: Task-level methodology drawing on 29,753 tasks in the Polish occupational classification, human survey/expert input and AI predictions, mapped to international occupations.  
Use in platform: Global exposure, exposure gradients, transformation-vs-replacement framing.

## S02 — NBER / Quarterly Journal of Economics
**Generative AI at Work**  
Date: 2023-04 (working paper; published 2025)  
Type: Field study  
Geography: Company-level customer support setting  
URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161  
Method note: Staggered introduction of an AI conversational assistant to 5,179 customer-support agents.  
Use in platform: Productivity heterogeneity by worker experience.

## S03 — NBER
**Still Waters, Rapid Currents: Early Labor Market Transformation under Generative AI**  
Date: 2025-05; revised 2026-03  
Type: Administrative records + adoption surveys  
Geography: Denmark  
URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w33777  
Method note: Links large-scale chatbot-adoption surveys to administrative labour-market records; difference-in-differences.  
Use in platform: Task reorganisation; earnings/hours null effects within two years.

## S04 — PwC
**2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer**  
Date: 2026-06-15  
Type: Job advertisement / company analysis  
Geography: Six continents  
URL: https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-jobs-barometer.html  
Method note: Analysis of more than one billion job ads plus company-level productivity/headcount analysis.  
Use in platform: AI-skill wage premium, junior-role seniorisation, professionalised vs democratised roles.

## S05 — Stanford Digital Economy Lab
**Canaries in the Coal Mine? Six Facts about the Recent Employment Effects of Artificial Intelligence**  
Date: 2026-08-12 revision  
Type: Administrative payroll analysis  
Geography: United States  
URL: https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/canaries-in-the-coal-mine-six-facts-about-the-recent-employment-effects-of-artificial-intelligence/  
Method note: ADP high-frequency administrative payroll data covering millions of US workers through June 2026.  
Use in platform: Young-worker employment patterns by AI exposure; no economy-wide displacement finding.

## S06 — OECD
**Young people, labour markets and AI: What OECD evidence shows**  
Date: 2026-08-15  
Type: Evidence synthesis / Employment Outlook context  
Geography: OECD countries  
URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/blogs/2026/08/young-people-labour-markets-and-ai-what-oecd-evidence-shows.html  
Method note: OECD interpretation of youth labour-market evidence and AI-related evidence.  
Use in platform: Caveat against attributing the broad youth-employment slowdown to AI.

## S07 — World Economic Forum
**Future of Jobs Report 2025**  
Date: 2025-01  
Type: Employer survey  
Geography: 55 economies  
URL: https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest/  
Method note: Survey of more than 1,000 employers representing over 14 million workers.  
Use in platform: Skills change, training needs, employer-reported barriers and responses.

## S08 — International Monetary Fund
**India: 2024 Article IV Consultation — Annex XIV: The Impacts of AI on India's Economy**  
Date: 2025  
Type: Country macro/labour assessment  
Geography: India  
URL: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/abstract/journals/002/2025/054/article-A001-en.xml

## S11 — International Labour Organization (ILO)
**Generative AI at work: What it means for jobs in Europe and beyond**  
Date: 2025  
Type: Nationally representative worker survey + focus groups  
Geography: Poland, with broader interpretation  
URL: https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/generative-ai-work-what-it-means-jobs-europe-and-beyond  
Method note: Focus groups with workers/managers plus nationally representative workplace adoption survey.  
Use in platform: Qualitative themes: verification, skill erosion concerns, consultation, governance gaps.

## S12 — International Labour Organization (ILO)
**The impact of GenAI on jobs, productivity and work organization: a review of the empirical evidence**  
Date: 2026-06-01  
Type: Empirical evidence review  
Geography: Multi-country  
URL: https://www.ilo.org/publications/impact-genai-jobs-productivity-and-work-organization-review-empirical  
Method note: Review of experiments, firm data, platform studies and representative surveys.  
Use in platform: Synthesis: productivity gains uneven; large-scale displacement limited; job-quality and young-worker risks.
